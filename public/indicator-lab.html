Scope and missing evidence

Current Nifty 200 constituents cause survivorship bias. All trades here are funded cash longs: five positions maximum, 20% allocation and 0.5% planned stop risk per stock, two per industry. Same-day rules use the previous daily candle, not five-minute indicators. Costs are scenarios, not broker quotes; personal income tax is excluded. Historical profit does not mean a rule always makes money.

Methods, all trade logs and reproduction

Initial stop: 2 prior ATR below the actual modeled entry. Indicator reversal: next open. Time exit: selected holding limit. No fixed profit target. Entry cancels outside +/-1 prior ATR of the signal close. Overnight gaps can exceed planned stop risk.

Supertrend uses Wilder ATR. HH/HL pivots are used only after two confirming bars. Ichimoku's visible cloud is shifted forward by 26 sessions; no future closes are read.
