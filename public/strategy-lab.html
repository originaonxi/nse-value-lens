Read the methodology and reproduce the experiments

Current stock constituents introduce survivorship bias. Daily OHLC cannot reconstruct every fill or short-sale restriction. ETF choices were added after the stock tests failed, introducing further selection bias. Futures use actual contracts and historical lots with full notional collateral; this is not a margin-account simulation.

The ETF trend model's earlier return changes from +0.15% to -9.17% at doubled costs. Its positive base results are not robust evidence. Gold buy-and-hold is a benchmark chosen with hindsight, not a discovered trading algorithm.
