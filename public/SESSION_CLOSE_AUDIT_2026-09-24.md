## Limits and remaining automation issue

- This was a manual recovery. It demonstrates working refresh/publication pipelines, **not successful scheduled triggering on this afternoon**. The workflows remain enabled; scheduled triggering has not been proved reliable by this recovery.
- VCP daily prices and ATR use 24 September. Its weekly candles and weekly screening remain through Friday 18 September because 24 September is a Thursday. Confirmed swing labels also require later candles and do not all change with every close.
- The homepage has usable strategy history for 199 stocks. ICICIAMC is excluded there because it has fewer than 210 complete bars; its latest price is still present in the 200-stock HH/HL and VCP views.
- Expanded, indicator, futures and cross-asset research reports remain dated 23 September. These are separate dated research outputs, not regenerated by the daily scanner jobs. The original VCP backtest report is also a dated historical artifact.
- Jev's Indian stock inputs use 24 September. US observations use their latest completed session; source gaps remain labelled. A fresh report does not mean every underlying source has the same observation date.
- Railway's homepage data had an additional cache delay after the fresh dataset was published. Its refresh behavior was changed to the scanners' one-minute server cache with a cache-busting upstream request, covered by a regression test.
