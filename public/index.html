    03 / THE PLAYBOOK

    One plan.
    No improvising.

    The scanner and backtest share the same rules. These are research hypotheses, not established NSE trading edges.

    Read methodology & repository review →
    1. Start with the market.

      New setups require Nifty above its 50-day average, which must be above its 200-day average. Otherwise stay in cash.

    2. Demand liquidity and a defined setup.

      At least ₹10 crore average daily turnover. Choose a breakout, a trend pullback, or a short mean-reversion setup.

    3. Plan the risk before the entry.

      Stop starts 2 ATR below the signal close. Enter only at the next open within ±0.5 ATR of that close. Check results dates, corporate actions and current quotes.

    4. Know when to leave.

      Exit at the stop, a 2R target, or the strategy's 5/10/20-session limit. A gap can cause a larger loss. Orders are not placed by this website.
