      11 / HOW TO READ THIS

      Greeks describe risk.
      Setups are fixed rules.

      DELTAHow much the option moves for a 1-point index move; roughly the model chance of finishing in the money.

      GAMMAHow fast delta changes. Highest at the money and near expiry.

      THETAPremium lost per calendar day if nothing else changes. Buyers pay it, sellers collect it.

      VEGAPremium change for a 1-point change in implied volatility.

      SIGMAOne standard deviation of the expected move implied by option prices; about two days in three should close inside it.

      PCRPut open interest divided by call open interest.
