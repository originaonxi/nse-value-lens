Data sources, glossary & limitations NSE UDiFF · F&O bhavcopy · caveats
DELTA
How much the option price moves per 1-point index move. Delta ≠ probability of profit.
GAMMA
Rate of delta change. Highest at-the-money near expiry.
THETA
Premium lost per calendar day. Buyers pay it; sellers collect it.
VEGA
Premium change per 1-point implied vol move. Long options gain on IV rise.
SIGMA (1σ)
One standard deviation expected move to expiry implied by options prices. ~68% of sessions should close within ±1σ over time.
PCR
Put OI ÷ Call OI. High PCR = more put hedging; not always bearish.
VRP
Implied vol minus realised vol. Negative = options look cheap vs recent moves. Not a reliable trading signal alone.
JEV CONFIDENCE
Model posterior probability on its own tier choice. Not a profit probability, not accuracy.
MATH SCORE
Heuristic: 50%·P(exit profit) + 30%·capped EV/risk + 20%·5th-pct downside buffer. Not a probability. Null if risk or exit data incomplete.
EXPIRY vs EXIT
Expiry payoff = theoretical gross P&L if held to contract expiry. Exit EV = model expected P&L at the planned 5-session close — what the plan actually targets.
