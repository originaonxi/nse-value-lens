Coverage & daily updates

15 widely followed commodity futures benchmarks and 11 major or commonly followed currency pairs. This is a selected watchlist, not a live ranking of global trading volume.

Commodity quotes are rolling futures, with units such as dollars per ounce or cents per bushel. Contract changes can affect chart patterns. Forex charts combine reported hourly prices into daily sessions ending at 17:00 New York time; USD/INR is an indicative global quote, not the RBI reference rate.

Only elapsed sessions are used. Incomplete or invalid records are excluded and flagged. When a download fails, the last saved chart stays visible with its original date and a Caution label. Forex trading volume is not supplied.

Automatic checks run Monday to Saturday at 04:35, 07:35 and 10:35 UTC. Holidays or provider delays can leave an older date visible. These price-structure labels are not a tested commodity or forex entry system.
